# ChurchCare organisational template library

Status: initial research catalogue and implementation specification. Templates marked draft are suggestions only; none is automatically applied to a church. Reviewed 31 October 2026.

## Product rule

These templates describe **organisational units and their relationships**, not the titles or appointments of the people who lead them.

A Region remains a Region regardless of whether its leader is called PICR, Regional Pastor, Bishop, Superintendent or another title. Leadership positions, office holders and granted authority are separate records.

Every adopting church may:
- preview a template before applying it;
- change level names, add/remove/reorder levels and adjust relationships before activation;
- keep its own customised copy without changing the shared template;
- submit proposed corrections to the shared template;
- receive template updates as suggestions, never silent overwrites.

Any change to an active church configuration must follow that church's granted-authority and approval rules. A suggested change does not alter live records.

## Template status meanings

- **Community-confirmed working template** — structure explicitly confirmed by a ChurchCare user for their own church context; not claimed to be universal or globally canonical.
- **Source-supported draft** — organisational terms and at least some relationships supported by a published source; still requires denomination/community review before being promoted.
- **Vocabulary only / sequence unverified** — terms are observed, but the complete parent-child order is not sufficiently established.
- **Research pending** — do not offer as a ready-made hierarchy yet.

## Initial catalogue

### 1. RCCG — Redeemed Christian Church of God

Status: **Community-confirmed working template**, based on the user's RCCG usage.

Working sequence, highest to lowest:

1. National
2. Region
3. Province
4. Zone
5. Area
6. Parish

Important qualification: this is the configuration confirmed by the ChurchCare user for the RCCG context they know. It intentionally omits Continent. RCCG's published 2026/2027 directory currently describes continental overseers as well as regions and provinces. Therefore ChurchCare must not present this user's configuration as the single universal RCCG structure. The adopting RCCG church may customise it to its actual context.

Source: https://www.rccg.org/our-structure/ (published 2026/2027 directory; accessed 2026-10-31).

### 2. Assemblies of God Nigeria

Status: **Source-supported draft**.

Suggested local working sequence:

1. General Council (national fellowship/governing body)
2. District
3. Section
4. Branch

A Lagos West District source describes 21 Sections and over 100 Branches, and explains that the district was established with General Council approval. Treat this as a source-supported Nigerian district example, not proof that every district or affiliated congregation uses an identical pattern. The template must permit autonomous or differently affiliated local assemblies.

Sources:
- https://www.aognigeria.org/
- https://www.aglagoswest.org/about
- https://www.aglagoswest.org/sections
- https://ag.org/About/About-the-AG/Structure (Assemblies of God USA source used only as background on fellowship/autonomy, not as evidence for Nigerian level names).

### 3. Nigerian Baptist Convention

Status: **Source-supported draft; cooperative affiliation model**.

Observed organisational units:
- Nigerian Baptist Convention
- Conference
- Association
- Local Baptist Church

Do not automatically treat this as a strict command hierarchy. The Convention constitution describes Associations as geographically contiguous groups of local churches, Conferences as wider geographical groupings of associations, and expressly recognises the autonomy of cooperating local Baptist churches. ChurchCare should model the relationship as a configurable affiliation/cooperation structure, with local church autonomy preserved.

Sources:
- https://nigerianbaptist.org/our-constitution/
- https://www.nigerianbaptist.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Nigerian-Baptist-Convention-Constitution1.pdf

### 4. Methodist Church Nigeria

Status: **Source-supported draft**.

Suggested sequence:

1. Conference
2. Archdiocese
3. Diocese
4. Circuit
5. Local Church

The World Council of Churches profile describes the Conference as the overall governing body, eight Archdioceses, Dioceses within each Archdiocese, and Circuits within Dioceses. Local church participation is also described. Confirm current names and any local variations before promoting this template.

Source: https://www.oikoumene.org/member-churches/methodist-church-nigeria

### 6. Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries (MFM)

Status: **Vocabulary only / sequence unverified**.

These are descriptions of geographic spread, not a confirmed nested hierarchy. Start with a Headquarters + Branch Network pattern only if the adopting church confirms it; do not invent intermediate levels.

### 8. Christ Apostolic Church (CAC)

Status: **Vocabulary only / sequence unverified; current constitutional review needs confirmation**.

- General Executive Council

- District Coordination Council (DCC)

### 10. Catholic Church in Nigeria

Status: **Research pending**.

## Leadership and appointment separation

Never add pastoral designations to the organisational level list. Examples of separate leadership-position records include:
- General Overseer
- PICR / Pastor in Charge of Region
- PICP / Pastor in Charge of Province
- Bishop
- Archbishop
- District Superintendent
- Sectional Leader
- Parish Pastor
- Vicar
- Circuit Superintendent

A position may be attached to an organisational unit. Its holder, title, appointment dates and authority are managed separately. Changing an office holder must not rename or recreate the organisational unit.

## Required data model for templates

Each template should store:
- stable template ID and denomination display name;
- country/region applicability;
- status and verification date;
- source URLs and short source notes;
- ordered organisational level definitions;
- accepted aliases/common names for each level;
- relationship model (strict parent-child, cooperative affiliation, network, or mixed);
- optional/required level flags;
- local-variation notes;
- template version and change history.

A church's adopted configuration is a **private copy** of the template plus its own customisations. Updating the shared template only creates an update suggestion for existing churches. It never overwrites their active configuration.

## Implementation guardrails

1. Do not treat any template as a fixed system enum.
2. Do not infer leadership titles from unit names.
3. Support both hierarchical parent-child structures and cooperative/network affiliations.
4. Allow an independent local church with no parent.
5. Allow a branch/unit to join its correct parent later, within the same church identity, with approval and preserved records.
6. Any user may submit a suggestion; only appropriately authorised reviewers can approve/publish changes.
7. Record who proposed, reviewed, approved and applied every change.
8. Do not silently delete or merge units when a level is changed.
9. Never let one church's customisation modify another church's template copy.
10. Keep uncertain templates visibly marked as drafts or under review.
